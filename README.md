# Customer Service Impact on Retention: A Call Data Study

This repository contains the Milestone 1 report for analyzing how customer service performance affects retention at Flipkart, based on call data. The study identifies operational gaps in support operations to reduce churn and improve satisfaction.

## Project Overview
- **Objective**: Evaluate customer support's role in retention by examining CSAT scores, response times, call durations, sentiments, and complaint patterns across call centers and channels.
- **Data Source**: CSV dataset of customer calls, cleaned and analyzed in Excel.
- **Key Sections**:
  - **Introduction**: Focus on service quality and retention.
  - **User Journey Map**: Bullet-point flow from purchase to issue resolution.
  - **Metric Tree**: Illustrates how CSAT drives retention, influenced by response time, call duration, and sentiments.
  - **Key Metrics**: CSAT, sentiments, response time, call duration, complaint distribution.
  - **Data Cleaning**: Handled missing values, duplicates, formats; derived columns like call date and day of week.
  - **Analysis & Insights**: 10 hypotheses tested (e.g., low CSAT in Kolkata, billing issues dominate at 72%, weekends show higher complaints).
  - **Recommendations**: Improve underperforming centers (e.g., Kolkata), optimize workloads in Delhi/Mumbai, enhance billing systems, strengthen channels like chatbots, adaptive scheduling.

## Files
- `Milestone-1_Report.pdf`: Full report with details, hypotheses, and visualizations.

## Key Findings
- Kolkata has the lowest CSAT; focus on training.
- Billing questions are 72% of complaints and lowest CSAT.
- Weak correlations between response time/call duration and CSAT; resolution quality matters more.
- Higher complaints on weekends/Saturdays; adjust staffing.
